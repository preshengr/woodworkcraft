About Us

Passionate woodworkers serving Ibadan & Abuja

Who We Are

Woodwork & Craft Ltd is a professional woodworking company specialising in all kinds of wood works. From elegant furniture and kitchen cabinets to library cabinets, doors, wine bars and more — we bring your ideas to life with skill and care.

With offices in Ibadan and Abuja, we proudly serve homes, offices and businesses across Nigeria with premium materials and expert craftsmanship.

What We Do

  • Custom & ready-made furniture
  • Kitchen & bathroom cabinets
  • Library & display cabinets
  • Wooden & carved doors
  • Wine bars & cellar units
  • Wardrobes, ceilings & all woodwork services
